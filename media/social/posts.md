### LinkedIn

> Two weeks ago my wife got dengue.
>
> I saw that all the hospitals were full of patients. People are dying. Dhaka is in the middle of its dengue season and the mosquitoes are breeding in water around our own homes: buckets, tyres, AC trays, flower pots, rooftops.
>
> While I sat with her, I built DengueWatch BD, an open-source web app:
> • Anyone can photograph standing water and pin it on a map in under 30 seconds. No app, no sign-up.
> • Volunteers ("hunters") claim a spot nearby, destroy it, and prove it with an on-site photo. Fake cleanups are reversed by moderators.
> • Everything is public: an open map, ward scorecards, and free anonymised data downloads.
>
> I don't know if it will help. But I did my part. The rest depends on people, and on our city corporations.
>
> If you're in Dhaka: check your roof and balcony today, and report one spot.

## 3 · For city corporations · `govt-en.mp4`

### LinkedIn

> An open invitation to DNCC, DSCC and every ward councillor in Dhaka.
>
> DengueWatch BD is an open-source citizen project. Citizens photograph standing water, volunteers clean it up with an on-site photo as proof, and everyone can see the progress. Today, volunteers run it. No government body is involved yet.
>
> What a city corporation would get, at no licence cost:
> • Structured reports: photo, GPS, container type, larvae seen; duplicates within 25 m are merged
> • An open map with ward-level reports, an environmental risk layer and official case numbers
> • A public scorecard per ward: places found, cleaned, and how fast
> • If you choose: a ward admin and inspectors per ward, each with their own work list; a place is only closed with an on-site photo
> • A weekly summary for ward admins, and free anonymised CSV/GeoJSON downloads
>
> Satellites can't see containers; the risk layer only shows where to look first. The real work is people on the ground, and right now those people are volunteers.
>
> If you'd like to add your ward, write to me at ashik.elahi.cse@gmail.com or use the contact page: https://dengue-watch-bd.vercel.app/contact
> Code: github.com/scornik/Dengue-Watch-BD
>
> (AI voice; app screens use demo data.)
>
> #Dengue #PublicHealth #SmartCity #Dhaka #Bangladesh #OpenData #CivicTech
